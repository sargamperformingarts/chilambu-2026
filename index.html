About Chilambu

Chilambu is a dance drama in Bharatanatyam, traditional folk, and semiclassical styles depicting Kannagi's story, 'The tale of an anklet,' from the ancient Tamil epic Shilappadikaram.

After the unjust Pandya king wrongfully executes her husband Kovalan for a theft he did not commit, Kannagi proves his innocence with her matching anklet. In a fit of righteous fury, she curses the king and the entire city of Madurai, leading to its fiery destruction. The goddess Meenakshi intervenes, Kannagi's grief and rage are pacified, and she is honored as a loving mother goddess. Her story is a powerful tale of fidelity, justice, and retribution, and she is revered in Tamil culture as a symbol of feminine empowerment and morality.
