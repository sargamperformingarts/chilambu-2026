Thank You

The curtain has fallen on Chilambu, and the last anklet bell has gone quiet. What stays with us is the audience that filled the hall.

Chilambu · October 25, 2026 · Etta & Joseph Miller Performing Arts

Thank you for attending, and for meeting Kannagi’s story with such stillness and warmth. A dance drama lives only as long as someone is watching it, and on October 25 you gave this one a full and attentive house.

To our dancers, thank you for the discipline behind every step. To the families behind them, thank you for the rides, the costume pins, and the patience. And to everyone who helped the evening happen, on stage and off, thank you.

The Audience For your time, your attention, and your applause.
The Dancers For months of rehearsal behind one evening.
Parents & Families For everything that happens before the stage lights.

Anupama Sankaranarayana Pillai
